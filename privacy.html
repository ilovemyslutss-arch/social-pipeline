Social Pipeline: Privacy Policy

Social Pipeline runs only on its owner's computer. It does not collect, sell or share personal data.

When you connect a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account, the tool stores the access token locally on that computer and uses it only to upload the videos you approve and to read basic profile and post statistics for your own account.

No data from other users is accessed. You can revoke access at any time in your platform account settings, which makes the stored token stop working.
